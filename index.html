How to Play

Battle RoyaleJump from the plane, loot buildings, stay inside the blue zone. Last one standing wins.

Move (touch)Drag anywhere on the left half. Push past the ring = auto-sprint. Double-tap & hold = sprint.
Aim (touch)Drag anywhere on the right half of the screen. Fire button can be held for full-auto.
Crouch / ProneTap CROUCH to crouch. Hold CROUCH 0.4s to go prone / stand up.
GrenadesTap NADE to throw. Double-tap NADE to switch frag ↔ smoke.
